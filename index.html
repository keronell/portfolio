Machine Learning / Computer Vision

Florence-2 → YOLOv8 Detection Pipeline

Problem
Training a detector needs labelled data, and hand-annotating a large image set is the bottleneck.
Approach
Ran Florence-2 zero-shot detection over Flickr30k images, filtered low-confidence predictions into a clean COCO dataset, converted to YOLO format, then trained YOLOv8 on person and pet (dog, cat, horse).
Results
A no-augmentation baseline and a rotation-plus-mixup run both peak at mAP@0.5 0.896; on the stricter mAP@0.5:0.95 the augmented model scored 0.687 against the baseline's 0.712. Heavier augmentation bought no accuracy here - on model-generated labels, label noise dominates. A CLAHE, sharpening and gamma stage at inference lifted the sample detection from 0.92 to 0.97.

Machine Learning / Computer Vision

Lab-to-Field Generalization in Leaf Disease Detection

Problem
Classifiers trained on controlled laboratory leaf images collapse on real field photographs - a domain shift problem, not an accuracy one.
Approach
Benchmarked EfficientNet-B0 and Vision Transformer backbones across PlantVillage, PlantDoc and FieldPlant, then applied supervised domain adaptation (quick-wins and progressive fine-tuning) and teacher-student pseudo-labelling for the unlabelled field case.
Results
Measured with macro-F1 across all three datasets - 61,486 lab images over 39 classes against 5,170 field images - including a pseudo-label confidence ablation at T = 0.85 / 0.90 / 0.95. Written up as an accompanying article.

Applied ML / Distributed Services

The Next Step - Career Matcher

Problem
Students choosing a technical direction get generic advice, disconnected from the skills employers are actually advertising for.
Approach
An adaptive questionnaire feeds a PyTorch scoring ensemble over questionnaire, semantic-similarity and skill-overlap features. Sentence Transformers and ChromaDB embed scraped job ads offline and retrieve them per career field at request time. Five FastAPI services sit behind an nginx gateway, connected with Dapr invocation and pub/sub over Redis state.
Results
Ranks 16 technical careers with the specific reasons behind each match and the skills held versus missing, then opens a skill-graph roadmap where every node carries how often real job ads demand it.

Software Engineering / Backend

ApprovalFlow - Invoice & Expense Approval

Problem
Invoice approval either burns reviewer time on trivial items or hands the decision to a model nobody can audit afterwards.
Approach
An AI agent recommends and a deterministic router decides: the low-risk majority auto-approves under a provable autonomy ceiling, anything unclear or high-value escalates to a human. Dapr Workflow runs the payment saga and the durable human-in-the-loop step over RabbitMQ pub/sub and per-service Redis state.
Results
Four journeys verified green end to end, 11/11 checks: auto-approve to paid, escalate-and-resume surviving a service restart, duplicate short-circuit, and payment failure with compensation. Every decision is traceable by a single correlation id.

Software Engineering / Full-Stack

AI Travel Planner

Problem
An LLM will happily invent a route that does not exist on any road or trail, and says nothing about the weather you would ride it in.
Approach
A Next.js 14 / React app generates multi-day cycling and trekking itineraries with an LLM, then snaps every leg to real roads and trails through OpenRouteService and renders it on Leaflet. A separate Express.js auth server handles bcrypt hashing, JWT issuance and silent refresh-token rotation, enforced by Next.js middleware.
Results
Produces routed multi-day plans with a three-day forecast for the area, persisted per user in MongoDB with saved history.
